## Elevation data
出典：国土地理院 標高タイル（<https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html>）を加工して作成
Source: elevation tiles of the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI), processed by this project.

- Layers: `dem5a` (5 m, zoom 15), `dem` (10 m, zoom 14) and `dem` (zoom 11).
- Used under the GSI content terms of use (国土地理院コンテンツ利用規約), which are compatible with CC BY 4.0:
  <https://www.gsi.go.jp/kikakuchousei/kikakuchousei40182.html>
- `public/content/baishinji/terrain_near.json`, `terrain_far.json` and `anchors.json` are derived from them.
- The novel's edits are baked into the height grid at load time: the rail bed, the shrine lane, the cutting
  for the hill tram, the summit and the cliff at the rock.
- The sea depth is invented (it grows with the distance from the shore). It is not survey data.

## Sun and moon
The sun position follows the NOAA solar position equations (public domain). The moon is approximate.
